What Is New

Season 2 is live

The VOA have taken Lyssara. Jules and her resistance are fighting to take it back, and the island has changed with them.

A bigger island

The map now runs 2.8 km across, with new named locations and new routes between them.

Map

Frosted Front is now on the map

A flash frozen wasteland left behind by the VOA's Biome Controller explosion. The resistance holds it now.

The Resistance

Jules' base of operations and her growing team, built to hit the VOA directly and liberate the island.

Homestead has been rebuilt

Once the comforting home of many Lyssara residents, it is now an industrial jungle running under VOA control.

Mended Meadows is out of VOA hands

The island's oldest landmark was recently liberated and is now undergoing repairs.

Gameplay

Vehicles across the island

Trucks and sedans now spawn along the roads, so a long rotation across the map is no longer a walk.

Stamina

Sprinting now draws from a stamina meter, so chasing a fight across open ground costs you something.

Exploration Mode

Take the island at your own pace and see what Season 2 changed without a match running around you.

UI

Custom HUD

Health, shield and stamina now read on a bar layout built for Odyssey instead of the default one.

Performance

World streaming

Ongoing work on how the island loads in and what it keeps in memory, aimed at fewer hitches when you cross between regions.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where an item granter handed out one copy of its item per player in the match instead of a single item.
  • Fixed the HUD bars reading fuller than your real health and shield when the values were low.
Known issue: footstep audio can go silent for the rest of a match. This is a Fortnite engine bug rather than something in the island, so it cannot be patched from my side. The workaround and the full list live on the known issues page.
